# Probability and Statistics

> Life is uncertain, and so is nature.

Probability enables us to quantify or model uncertain phenomena or behaviors, make predictions of likelihood of future events and reason about these phenomena and events.

While modeling such phenomena, many parameters may be unknown to us. In order to learn about the parameters that describe their nature, we need to collect data from the real-world. Statistics provides us with techniques to collect, analyze and interpret past numerical data from some samples to inferring (estimate) the parameters representative of the whole data. 

Probability allows to study the intrinsic randomness of many such naturally stochastic phenomena, while statistics allows to infer the model's parameters, as well as understanding underlying process that explains the data.
